A place for curious minds and bold ideas.

TechNova is a plateform where innovation meets creativity and technology brings ideas to life. Our college's annual technical fest brings together passionate students, developers, and technology enthusiasts to explore new ideas, showcase their skills, and compete through exciting challenges.

Who we are

TechNova is more than just a technical festival; it is a celebration of knowledge, creativity, and collaboration. Organized by our college, TechNova provides students with opportunities to learn, experiment, and demonstrate their technical abilities.

Through competitions, workshops, and interactive activities, we encourge students to think beyond classrooms and turn their innovative ideas into reality.
